Final Quest: The Last Bus 🚌

Read the original passage. Then show that you can explain how figurative language shapes meaning and tone.

The Last Bus

Jalen burst through the school doors and froze. The bus loop was almost empty. His bus sat at the far end, its red lights blinking like impatient eyes. “No, no, no,” he whispered. His backpack felt as heavy as a refrigerator as he took off running.

Thump, thump, thump! His sneakers pounded the sidewalk. The distance between him and the bus seemed to stretch forever. Then the doors began to close.

“Wait!” Jalen shouted.

The driver spotted him and stopped. The doors opened again. Relief washed over him like a cool wave. He climbed aboard, dropped into the nearest seat, and grinned. “Piece of cake,” he said between gasps.
